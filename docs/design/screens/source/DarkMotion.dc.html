Motion rules

Durations, easing and limits used everywhere.

150ms hover and press · 220ms panels, steps, toasts · 250ms summary sheet enter · 600ms numbers and bars arriving.

One easing everywhere: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), fast start, soft landing.

Motion only explains change: something arrived, moved, was removed or can be undone. Nothing loops for attention; the mascot’s breathing is the only idle motion.

Reduced motion: everything becomes an instant cross-fade, the mascot holds still.
