Pochimail

A private assistant for the email accounts you already have. It shows the big picture of your mailboxes, flags what matters, proposes cleanups and saves your important documents.

Your email never leaves your computer.

The privacy promise

  • Your email stays on this computer. There is no Pochimail server.
  • Local AI only. The models run on your device and are downloaded once, checked against a checksum.
  • No telemetry and no crash reporting.
  • You decide. Nothing changes without your approval unless you turn on Autopilot, which only does high-confidence, low-risk actions and tells you afterwards.
  • No email content is stored: only ids, dates, counts and what Pochimail did.

The details, including exactly which Google data the app uses, are in the privacy policy.

About

Pochimail is not a mail client: any single email opens in your usual mail app. Version 1 supports Gmail. It is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), and it is in early development.
